85402ff

November 18, 2026
  • Added patch notes
  • Quick play now uses Tactical 2.0. The new bot keeps workers in production while separate factories send fighters and artillery forward. It spreads out its bases and sends search parties after surviving enemies. You can still choose Standard 1.0 in the game directory.
  • Artillery can spot enemies from 8 tiles away, up from 5. Its guns reach 10 tiles.
  • Ground units now see around corners only as far as their vision allows. Walls block direct sight, and previously seen buildings and units appear gray when you lose sight of their tiles.
  • Default matches are first to 3 points, down from 5. You can still choose a different target when setting up a game.
  • Try a different move in a recorded game. Pick a timeline and tick, choose “Practice as,” then “Play this timeline.” Your opponent follows their recorded plan while you try new orders in a private copy. The original result stays intact.
  • Inspect earlier timelines. Turn on the overlay to see orders and events at each tick. Spectators and players reviewing a finished game can inspect both sides’ orders and factory queues.
  • Revise a plan in fewer clicks. “Select all matching” expands your selection to matching unit and building types. “Clear all future orders” stages a reset after the selected tick. You can undo it before committing.
  • Queue missile salvos. Shift-click each destination to keep adding launches. If a silo would run out of that missile, it queues production to supply the launches.
